# Security Policy

## Supported versions

| Version | Supported |
| ------- | --------- |
| 1.x     | Yes       |

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately via GitHub Security Advisories on
[github.com/chayprabs/og-image-online](https://github.com/chayprabs/og-image-online/security/advisories/new).

Do not open public issues for undisclosed vulnerabilities.

We aim to acknowledge good-faith reports within a reasonable time and may
coordinate disclosure. We do not guarantee bounties, timelines, or specific
remediation outcomes unless expressly agreed in writing.

## Scope

SocialRender processes user content locally in the browser. Server-side
components are optional static hosting only.

**In scope:** vulnerabilities in this repository’s code, dependencies with
exploitable impact on users of the official build, and misconfigurations in
documented deployment paths.

**Out of scope:** social engineering, physical attacks, issues in third-party
sites linked from the header, and vulnerabilities requiring unrealistic user
actions without a demonstrated exploit chain.

## Safe harbor

Researchers who follow responsible disclosure and avoid privacy violations,
data destruction, or service disruption will not be pursued for good-faith
security research, to the extent permitted by law.

## Legal

Nothing in this policy creates a contract or obligation beyond what applicable
law requires. Use of the Service remains subject to the [Terms](legal/TERMS.md).
